# ☄️ Meteorite AR

**An immersive Android space-combat and survival game**  
**Developer:** EdWorld AE / ECAE Game Studios Productions  
**Inspected build:** `Meteorite-AR-0.66.apk`  
**Platform:** Android  
**Official distribution philosophy:** Free to play, no ads, no subscriptions

## About the game

Meteorite AR combines arcade-style spacecraft combat with camera-overlay flight and immersive space environments. Pilot a customizable ship, evade meteorites and hostile craft, collect power-ups, improve your equipment, fight increasingly formidable bosses, and explore a five-chapter science-fiction campaign.

The game is designed around accessible entertainment: its official release is intended to be free, without advertising or mandatory subscriptions. Its fictional story features exaggerated characters and cosmic conflicts.

## Game modes

- **Campaign:** A five-chapter, 25-level storyline featuring progressively stronger adversaries and sector commanders.
- **Survival:** Endless 60-second stages without bosses, with additional meteorites and enemies introduced each minute.
- **Free roaming:** An exploration-oriented flight option with panoramic environments.
- **Wi-Fi co-op:** A pairing and shared-flight interface, including a co-op hangar, profile names, and teammate revival mechanics.

## Flight environments and controls

The settings interface provides several ways to experience flight:

- Live rear-camera background with tilt steering.
- Live rear-camera background with joystick-only controls.
- Frozen camera frame with joystick steering.
- Random space backgrounds with joystick steering.
- 3D panoramic backgrounds for free roaming and randomized panoramic backgrounds between levels.
- External spacecraft and first-person combat perspectives.

Named space environments include **Stellar Jet, Earthrise, Ember Nebula, Violet Nebula, Stellar Pillar, Blue Haze, Galactic Core, Deep Starfield,** and **Dark Nebula**.

The interface includes fire controls, pause/resume, recenter/calibrate, an end-flight action, and a salvage-pulse action. Players can configure joystick placement, crosshair shape, crosshair size and color, interface color, music, and sound effects.

## The five campaign chapters

| Chapter | Levels | Section | Principal antagonist |
|---|---:|---|---|
| I | 1–5 | The Galactic Quarantine | The Walrus King |
| II | 6–10 | The Antagonistic Emissary | EdWorld AE |
| III | 11–15 | The Vicious Emperor | Viclouis |
| IV | 16–20 | The Origin Directive | Zukerberg |
| V | 21–25 | Beyond Existence | Elon Tusk |

### Chapter I — The Galactic Quarantine

Humanity discovers that the apparent silence of the universe conceals an enforced quarantine. The Walrus King sends Federation commanders to prevent Earth from advancing beyond an imposed technological limit.

### Chapter II — The Antagonistic Emissary

EdWorld AE dispatches enemies who observe, adapt to, calculate, and condition the pilot's behavior. The campaign becomes a contest of tactics and prediction.

### Chapter III — The Vicious Emperor

Viclouis and the Crimson Dominion introduce a more destructive imperial force powered by collapsed stars. Their conflict exposes hints about the Federation's hidden knowledge of humanity.

### Chapter IV — The Origin Directive

Zukerberg, the Architect of Reality, and his Reality Enforcers manipulate space, repeated attacks, and possible outcomes as the pilot approaches the Origin Nexus.

### Chapter V — Beyond Existence

Elon Tusk, ruler of the Primordial Dominion, sends four Transcendent Avatars to evaluate Earth. The final confrontation places humanity's future across multiple worlds at stake.

**Fiction disclaimer:** All characters and events in the game, including those inspired by real people, are fictional.

## Combat, pickups, and progression

The packaged flight manual documents these systems:

- **Ship health and wings:** Each wing adds 100 hull HP. Hull upgrades add 50 or 150 HP.
- **Weapons:** Laser upgrades increase base laser damage by 25% or 50%. A blue laser pickup temporarily enables 50-damage lasers.
- **Defense:** Damage Resistance upgrades reduce incoming damage by 25%, 50%, or 75%, depending on tier. Speed pickups can grant a non-recharging 100-HP shield.
- **Special attack:** Collect a turquoise `U` pickup to activate the Unibeam area attack.
- **Auto Pilo AI:** Optional automatic steering away from incoming fire and forward firing.
- **Auto Aim:** Optional targeting that prioritizes nearby meteorites and then hostile ships.
- **Salvage relay:** Collect gold rings for temporary speed boosts; complete a three-ring chain to earn coins and repair HP. Qualified chains can charge a salvage pulse that clears threats and delays enemy fire.
- **Boss mechanics:** Shield phases, exposed weak points, reactor vulnerability windows, meteor showers, and cooperative shield-breaking opportunities.
- **Optional objectives:** Certain sector objectives provide additional currency rewards.

The game also references the **Inglorious Quartet**, a four-ship formation unlocked through a complete trio of upgrades.

## In-game economy

The APK contains several progression and purchasing interfaces:

- **Space Coins:** The flight manual describes passive flight earnings and rewards for destroying meteorites and enemies.
- **Crystal tokens:** An inventory currency that can be exchanged for Space Coins.
- **Federation Shop:** Animated hull finishes and a cargo exchange.
- **Upgrade shop:** Persistent equipment improvements.
- **Shipyard:** Ship preview, purchase, and equip controls.

These are described as **in-game currencies and systems**; their presence does not establish real-money purchases. The official game's stated policy is free access without pay-to-win mechanics.

## Cooperative play

The packaged interface and code include Wi-Fi pairing, a co-op profile name, a shared flight state, a co-op hangar, and cooperative combat logic. The flight manual describes shared pause behavior, alternating attacks on boss shields, and one field revival per level restoring 50 HP.

Actual multiplayer connectivity and device compatibility should be verified through testing on two Android devices.

## Leaderboards and saved progress

The game provides leaderboard categories for campaign, survival, and Wi-Fi co-op. Local storage and save-related logic appear in the packaged application. The factory-reset dialog states that a reset erases wallets, upgrades, campaign progress, leaderboards, settings, and Wi-Fi pairing information stored on the device.
