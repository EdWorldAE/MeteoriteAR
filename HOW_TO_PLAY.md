# ☄️ Meteorite AR — How to Play & Gameplay Mechanics

**Official title:** Meteorite AR  
**Internal abbreviation:** Meteor AR  
**Developer:** EdWorld AE · ECAE Game Studios Productions  
**Guide based on:** Android APK v0.66 and its packaged Flight Manual

> **Your mission:** Pilot a spacecraft through hostile space, survive incoming hazards, earn Space Coins, strengthen your ship, and defeat the commanders standing between humanity and freedom.

## 1. Start your flight

1. Launch **Meteorite AR**.
2. Choose **Campaign**, **Survival**, **Free Roaming**, or **Wi-Fi Co-op**.
3. Choose whether to **Launch with camera** or **Play without camera**, where offered.
4. In **Settings**, configure your flight background, steering, combat perspective, crosshair, and joystick position.
5. Start a mission. Stay mobile, fire at threats, collect pickups, and watch your hull health.

**First-flight recommendation:** Start in Campaign with a joystick-controlled space background. After learning the controls, experiment with live-camera AR, tilt steering, and panoramic flight.

## 2. Flight controls

| Control | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **Joystick** | Steer your spacecraft when joystick steering is selected. |
| **Device tilt** | Steer using the phone's motion in supported camera/tilt mode. |
| **Fire** | Shoot at incoming meteorites and hostile spacecraft. |
| **⏸ Pause** | Pause or resume combat. In Wi-Fi co-op, either pilot can pause the shared flight. |
| **Reca** | Recenter the joystick or recalibrate tilt controls. |
| **End** | Leave the current flight and return to the menu. |
| **RP** | Release a charged salvage pulse. |
| **Fire Unibeam** | Trigger the special area attack after collecting the turquoise **U** pickup. |

Control placement and appearance can vary with the selected settings and combat view.

### Flight backgrounds and perspectives

Meteorite AR supports live rear-camera flight with tilt or joystick steering, a frozen camera frame, randomized space backgrounds, and 3D panoramic environments. Select **External ship view** to see your craft or **First person combat** for a cockpit-oriented perspective. Panoramic environments support looking and traveling around the surrounding space scene.

## 3. Choose your mode

### 🎯 Campaign — Fight through the story

The campaign contains **25 levels across five chapters**. Survive each level's timed combat section, then defeat the boss when it appears to advance. Different enemies introduce new patterns, hazards, and tactics.

| Chapter | Levels | Main antagonist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I — The Galactic Quarantine | 1–5 | The Walrus King |
| II — The Antagonistic Emissary | 6–10 | EdWorld AE |
| III — The Vicious Emperor | 11–15 | Viclouis |
| IV — The Origin Directive | 16–20 | Zukerberg |
| V — Beyond Existence | 21–25 | Elon Tusk |

**Tip:** Finish optional sector objectives before the boss arrives to receive **15 bonus Space Coins**. Objectives can involve clearing lane targets, passing through signals, or destroying a marked ace.

### ♾️ Survival — Last as long as possible

Survival consists of endless **60-second stages without bosses**. Every minute adds **one meteor and one enemy** to the horde. As you progress, enemies become more tactical: beyond stage 25, pilots may flank, lead moving targets, and dodge shots. Watch for **amber nose lights**, which indicate an imminent enemy shot.

### 🌌 Free Roaming — Explore the environment

Fly in the game's immersive environments without following the standard campaign progression. Experiment with ship movement, camera perspectives, and 3D panoramic space scenes.

### 🤝 Wi-Fi Co-op — Fly together

Use the co-op pairing interface and hangar to join a shared flight. Configure a co-op profile name in Settings. Either pilot can pause. Coordinate attacks against boss shields: **alternating shield hits within 0.85 seconds** increases shield damage. One field revival per level can restore **50 HP**.

## 4. Understand combat

### Meteorites, enemies, and hazards

- **Meteorites:** Dodge them or destroy them with your weapons.
- **Enemy ships:** Avoid incoming fire while returning fire; later enemies can adapt their approach.
- **Artillery:** Watch for marked blast radii and move out of the danger zone.
- **Support ships:** Some protect nearby fighters, making target selection important.
- **Scrap and derelict ships:** These travel through their lanes; steer clear.
- **Meteor showers:** Boss encounters can unleash a dangerous wave of rocks.

### Boss fights

At the end of a campaign level's timer, the boss must be defeated to advance.

1. Avoid the boss's incoming fire and meteor showers.
2. Shoot its shield or survive until its shield phase ends.
3. Watch for a **lit weak point** after a volley: hits there deal **triple hull damage**.
4. Breaking the shield can expose the reactor for **three seconds**, during which hull damage is doubled.
5. Use the **Unibeam** when available for area damage.

**Packaged Flight Manual values:** Standard laser hits deal **10 hull damage** or **25 shield damage** in the documented boss system. A boss shield has **100 points** or can time out after **10 seconds**. The Unibeam deals **50 area damage**. Bosses from level 11 begin at **5,000 HP**, increasing by **1,000 per level**; final commanders have **19,000 HP**.

## 5. Pickups and special mechanics

| Pickup or mechanic | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Blue L pickup** | Enables **50-damage lasers for 4 seconds**. |
| **Turquoise U pickup** | Makes **Unibeam** available; aim and press **Fire Unibeam**. |
| **Speed pickup** | Grants a **100-HP shield** that does not recharge. |
| **Gold salvage ring** | Grants **1.625× forward speed for 2 seconds**. |
| **Three-ring chain** | Awards **12 coins** and repairs **15 HP**. Missing a ring breaks the chain. |
| **Salvage pulse** | Charge by passing through the glowing cores of **two rings within a completed chain**; press **RP** to activate. |
| **Violet wormhole** | On even-numbered levels, may appear during the first minute; flying through its center advances the level timer by up to **30 seconds**. |

A salvage pulse **clears incoming bolts, destroys nearby meteorites, and delays enemy fire**. You can hold **one pulse at a time**. A shield speed boost takes priority at **2.6× speed**.

**Tactical combination:** Chain salvage rings to recover health and charge RP, then save the pulse for a dense wave of projectiles or meteorites.

## 6. Health, upgrades, and automation

### Armor and weapons

- **Wings:** Each wing adds **100 hull HP**. Losing 100 HP removes the earliest purchased wing.
- **Hull upgrades:** Add **50 HP** or **150 HP**, depending on the upgrade.
- **Laser upgrades:** Increase base laser damage by **25%** or **50%**.
- **Damage Resistance:** Reduces incoming damage by **25%, 50%, or 75%** according to tier, before shields and hull absorb the remaining hit.
- **Inglorious Quartet:** A complete trio of the specified advanced upgrades unlocks a **four-ship square formation with 400 base HP**.

### Assisted flight

- **Auto Pilo AI:** Automatically steers away from incoming fire and fires forward.
- **Auto Aim:** Targets the nearest meteorite first, then the nearest hostile ship.

Both systems can be toggled using the upgrade controls. Assisted systems can help manage threats, but do not replace awareness of hazards and boss mechanics.

## 7. Earn and spend Space Coins

The Flight Manual states that **every five seconds of active flight earns one Space Coin**. Additional rewards come from combat, objectives, and salvage-ring chains.

- **Coinspace Earner:** Doubles flight earnings.
- **Master:** Provides **4× base payout**.
- The game uses the strongest purchased earning tier rather than stacking both multipliers.
- **Crystal tokens:** Can be exchanged at the cargo exchange; **10 crystal tokens → 10 Space Coins**.

Spend earned currency on upgrades, ship designs, and available cosmetic content in the **Upgrade Shop**, **Shipyard**, and **Federation Shop**.

## 8. Customize your spacecraft

Visit the Shipyard through Settings to preview, purchase, and equip ships. The Flight Manual identifies ship designs as **cosmetic**.

Available designs include **Cadet**, **Vector Interceptor**, **Manta Wing**, **Twin-Lance Corvette**, **Aurora Crescent**, **Ember Trident**, **Prism Kite**, **Aster Cathedral**, **Helix Raptor**, and **Orbital Citadel**.

Personalization includes hull colors, animated hull finishes, joystick placement, crosshair shape/size/color, interface colors, and separate music/effects controls.

## 9. Strategy for new pilots

1. **Prioritize survival.** Learn how meteorites and projectiles approach before focusing on maximum damage.
2. **Keep moving.** Direction changes are especially useful against enemies that predict your flight path.
3. **Use Reca when needed.** Recenter or recalibrate before a difficult encounter.
4. **Collect salvage rings deliberately.** A full chain repairs your ship and earns currency.
5. **Preserve special attacks.** Unibeam and salvage pulses are most valuable during crowded combat moments.
6. **Target exposed boss weak points.** Timing matters more than indiscriminate firing.
7. **Invest in defenses and damage.** Hull, wings, resistance, and lasers improve survivability and damage output.
8. **Use co-op coordination.** Alternating attacks on boss shields and preserving the field revive can change the outcome of a battle.

## 10. Progress, settings, and reset

Meteorite AR includes leaderboards for **Campaign**, **Survival**, and **Wi-Fi Co-op**, as well as stored progression, currency, upgrades, and preferences.

**Warning:** The **Factory reset** option erases both wallets, upgrades, campaign progress, leaderboards, settings, and Wi-Fi pairing information stored on the device. Use it only when you intend to restart.
